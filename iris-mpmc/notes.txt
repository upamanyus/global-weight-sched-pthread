Architecture
------------

  • Ghost state:
      γq : excl_authR (listO valO)            — abstract queue
      γph : auth (gmap Z (excl val))          — push-inflight tokens
      γpp : auth (gmap Z (excl val))          — pop-inflight tokens

  • is_queue γq q cap holds (q +ₗ 2) ↦□ #slots persistently and the inv.

  • LP placement:
      - successful push: CAS on tail (vs becomes vs ++ [v]); pusher
        receives a γph fragment {[ pos := Excl v ]} as the obligation
        to publish (steps 2+3 of the impl).
      - successful pop: CAS on head (vs becomes tail vs); popper
        receives γpp fragment as obligation to advance the turn.
      - failure branches: commit AU with vs unchanged; spec permits
        spurious failures (no fullness/emptiness witness, which would
        need prophecy variables).

Bridging the two invariant openings (the line-630 admit)
--------------------------------------------------------

The argument:
  At load time (state '), we observe turn = exp_turn = 2*(head1/cap)+1
  at slot idx = head1 mod cap.  Slot_state forces case A2 (cases B1,
  B2, C are ruled out by parity / round arithmetic), giving p = head1
  and head' ≤ head1 < tail'.

  At CAS time (state ''), head'' = head1.  We need head1 < tail''.

  We have *neither* head nor tail monotonicity captured in the
  invariant.  Two options:
    (a) Add `own γtl (●MN tail) ∗ own γhd (●MN head)` to the invariant.
        Take a snapshot ◯MN (S head1) of γtl during the load opening
        (after deriving tail' ≥ S head1 via case A2).  Use it at the
        CAS opening to conclude tail'' ≥ S head1 > head1.
    (b) Use prophecy variables for the linearisation point of pop.

  Option (a) is the standard Iris idiom; option (b) is heavier.

Invariants to keep visible
--------------------------

  queue_inv_inner γq γph γpp q slots cap takes 7 existentials:
    head tail (nats), vs (list val), turns svals (list val),
    piph pipp (gmap Z val).

  Pure facts:
    head ≤ tail ≤ head + cap
    length turns = cap, length svals = cap
    length vs = tail - head
    dom piph ⊆ [head, tail), dom pipp ⊆ [0, head)

  Heap:
    (q +ₗ 0) ↦ #(Z.of_nat head)
    (q +ₗ 1) ↦ #(Z.of_nat tail)
    [∗ list] i ↦ tv ∈ turns, ⌜∃ z, tv = #z⌝ ∗ (slots +ₗ 2*i) ↦ tv
    [∗ list] i ↦ sv ∈ svals, (slots +ₗ 2*i+1) ↦ sv

  Ghost:
    own γq (●E vs)
    own γph (● (Excl <$> piph))
    own γpp (● (Excl <$> pipp))

  Pure slot-state coupling: ∀ i < cap, ∃ z, turns!!i = Some #z ∧
    slot_state cap head tail vs piph pipp i z (default #0 svals!!i).
